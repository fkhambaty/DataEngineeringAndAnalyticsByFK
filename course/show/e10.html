ADR 0005

Why scores stay in the warehouse.

The bank stays the bank

A model score is analytical output. Putting it in Postgres would make the warehouse a source of the system of record.

no score written to core

No loop

Warehouse, then model, then the OLTP, then the extractor, then the warehouse. A bad batch would copy itself before anyone looked.

one direction only

One feature table

Training and the offer both read ml_customer_features. Two definitions of the same feature is how a model that looked good offline fails live.

training and serving share it
MERIDIAN_AUC_GATE · default 0.70

Below the line, no scores.

gate 0.70  ·  the line, not a measured run
A coin toss sits at 0.50. A holdout with one class has no AUC, and that run fails the same gate.
BELOW 0.70 · REJECTED

The run is stored. Its scores are not. Marts keep the last good model. Offers fall back to the published policy.

0.70 OR ABOVE · SUCCEEDED

Scores land in MODEL_SCORES. score_source on the offer becomes MODEL.

Seed size, in the SQL

12,000 applications. 15 authorizations on each card. About 1% of swipes are injected patterns. That is the generator. It is not a warehouse total.

The policy is published

core.card_policy_logit. Probability 0.55 or above is APPROVED. The weights are teaching values. The model has to recover that policy. The label is a little noisy on purpose.
