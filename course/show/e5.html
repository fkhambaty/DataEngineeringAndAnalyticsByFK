snap_customers · snap_branches

strategy = timestamp

updated_at is the signal. The Postgres trigger bumps it on a real change, so a new segment opens a new version.

snap_accounts

strategy = check

Every payment bumps accounts.updated_at. A timestamp strategy would store a version per payment.
