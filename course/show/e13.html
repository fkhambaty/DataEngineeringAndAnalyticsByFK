01Isolation
A logical wall. One wrong CASE line shows another tenant. The policy must be tested, deny by default.
WINSAn object wall. A missing grant fails loud. It does not leak a neighbour’s rows.
02Versioning
WINSOne schema, one dbt deploy. Every tenant moves together. No drift to chase.
Each tenant can sit on its own version. N deploys, and the copies drift apart.
03Cost
WINSOne copy of each model, one build, one warehouse pool. Per-tenant bills need tags.
Every model built N times. More objects, storage, and runs. The bill splits by tenant easily.
04Blast radius
A bad deploy or a bad policy reaches every tenant at once.
WINSA broken build stays inside one tenant. A bad template still rolls out to all, one at a time.
Two all. The contract decides, not taste.Meridian is one bank with internal personas, so it ships shared marts with a row policy on the person. Switch to a database per tenant when a contract demands a physical wall, its own release clock, or its own bill.

The scar

One failure you fixed.

THE POOL

The DAG looked busy.

The tasks were waiting. Pool meridian_postgres did not exist yet. Read the task state, not the DAG state. Import creates four slots.

DAG running→pool: 4 slots
THE EXTRA ROWS

Raw was fatter than Postgres.

Bronze keeps every version on purpose. Staging keeps the newest. More rows in raw is the design, not a broken load.

raw > source→raw keeps every copy
ONE DAY

A single day would not match.

A rolling cutoff compares part of the oldest Postgres day with the whole Snowflake day. Both sides now share UTC dates and a two-minute cutoff.

partial day→same UTC day
THE POLICY

The lock refused a join.

A row-policy body cannot contain a JOIN. The fix is separate EXISTS checks. The person is CURRENT_USER(), not the role.

JOIN in policy→EXISTS + CURRENT_USER()
