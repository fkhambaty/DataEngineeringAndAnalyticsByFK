How an OLTP database is shaped

Built to run the bank,
not to understand it

✓
Normalised: each fact is stored exactly once, in its own table.
✓
Perfect for "show me account 4521, now": one row, instantly.
!
A business question means joining many tables and reading millions of rows.
Think of a bank counter: built to serve one customer at a time, fast. Not to count every customer in the country.

Meet the team

Every tool has one job

The till

PostgreSQL

Records every transaction as it happens. The source of truth.

The courier

Python

Carries only what changed since its last trip, then delivers it to Snowflake.

The warehouse

Snowflake

Stores everything. Storage and compute scale separately, and you pay per second.

The kitchen

dbt

SQL recipes turn raw ingredients into clean, tested, star-shaped tables.

The alarm clock

Airflow

Starts every job on time, in the right order. Retries, and raises the alarm.

The translator

Streamlit + Cortex

An English question becomes SQL, and the answer only shows what you're allowed to see.
